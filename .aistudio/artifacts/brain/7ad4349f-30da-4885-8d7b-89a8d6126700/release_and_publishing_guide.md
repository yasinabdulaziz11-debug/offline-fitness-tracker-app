# Offline Fitness Tracker — Download & Publishing Guide

---

## 1. How to View and Test the Running App Now
In your AI Studio interface shown in your screenshot:
- Tap the **"Preview"** tab located at the bottom center (between **Chat** and **Artifacts**).
- This launches the cloud-streaming Android emulator running **Offline Fitness Tracker** directly in your browser. You can start workouts, log sets, adjust the rest timer, view charts, and test all features live.

---

## 2. How to Download the App (APK / AAB) & Source Code
To export the project onto your local machine or phone:
1. Tap the **"..."** (three dots menu) on the bottom right of the AI Studio toolbar.
2. Choose your preferred export option:
   - **Download APK / AAB**: Generates the package ready for installation on Android devices or upload to the developer console.
   - **Export as ZIP**: Downloads the complete native Android project (Kotlin, Compose, Room, Gradle) to open in Android Studio.
   - **Push to GitHub**: Connects your repository for automated CI/CD and version control.

---

## 3. How to Publish on Google Play

### Step 1: Google Play Developer Account
1. Open [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Create or sign in to your developer account.
3. Click **Create App**:
   - **App name**: `Offline Fitness Tracker`
   - **Default language**: English (United States)
   - **App or game**: App
   - **Free or paid**: Free

### Step 2: Prepare Store Listing
- **Short Description (up to 80 chars)**:
  > Fast, private, 100% offline fitness, workout, and body measurement tracker.
- **Full Description**:
  > Take full control of your fitness journey without needing an account or internet connection. Offline Fitness Tracker is built for gym-goers, home-workout enthusiasts, and lifters who value privacy, speed, and reliability.
  >
  > Key Features:
  > • 100% Offline: Zero accounts, zero login, zero servers. Your data stays securely on your phone.
  > • Intuitive Workout Logger: Log weight, repetitions, duration, and distances across custom set types (Normal, Warm-up, Drop Sets, Failure).
  > • Built-in Auto Rest Timer: Automatic countdown with adjustable intervals (+15s, +30s) and haptic vibration feedback.
  > • 23+ Pre-Loaded Exercises & Custom Creator: Comprehensive exercises across Chest, Back, Legs, Arms, Core, Shoulders, and Cardio, plus form instructions.
  > • Reusable Workout Routines: Build custom training splits or use pre-configured starter templates.
  > • Personal Records & Estimated 1RM: Track your all-time heaviest lifts and estimated 1-rep max (Epley formula).
  > • Visual Progress Charts: Monitor workout volume trends over time on clean charts.
  > • Body Measurements: Log weight, waist, chest, arms, and thighs offline.
  > • Flexible Unit Support: Seamless toggle between Metric (kg, cm, km) and Imperial (lbs, in, mi).
  > • Safe Backup & Export: Export and import complete JSON backups or export CSV tables for workouts and measurements.
- **Category**: Health & Fitness
- **Tags**: Fitness, Workout Tracker, Gym Log, Bodybuilding, Exercise, Offline

### Step 3: App Content & Declarations
- **Privacy Policy**:
  > Provide a URL pointing to the offline privacy declaration (see Settings screen: No user data is collected, transmitted, or shared).
- **Target Audience**: 13+ (or General Audience).
- **Data Safety Form**:
  - Does your app collect or share any user data? **No**.
  - All data is stored locally in an on-device SQLite database.
- **Permissions Declaration**:
  - `VIBRATE`: Normal permission for timer haptic notification.
  - Zero storage or camera permissions required.

### Step 4: Upload Build & Release
1. In the Play Console sidebar, go to **Production** (or **Internal Testing** first).
2. Create a new release and upload the **Android App Bundle (`.aab`)** generated by AI Studio or Android Studio.
3. Review release notes and rollout!
