## 📸 Google Play Store Screenshots (1080 × 1920 HD PNG)

All screenshots are formatted in **1080 × 1920 px (9:16 portrait)** with the modern dark theme (`#101614` background, `#2EE59D` vibrant lime accents):

1. **`screenshots/screenshot_1_dashboard_1080x1920.png`**
   - **Title**: Home Dashboard & Streak
   - **Highlights**: Active 5-day streak fire badge, Quick Start Workout, weekly volume summary (14,250 kg), routine quick cards.

2. **`screenshots/screenshot_2_workout_logger_1080x1920.png`**
   - **Title**: Live Workout Logger & Rest Timer
   - **Highlights**: Real-time set logging (reps, kg, RPE), set completion toggles, interactive floating rest countdown timer pill (`01:30`).

3. **`screenshots/screenshot_3_exercise_library_1080x1920.png`**
   - **Title**: Exercise Library & Filtering
   - **Highlights**: 25+ preloaded exercises categorized by muscle groups (Chest, Back, Legs, Shoulders, Arms, Core), equipment filter chips, Personal Record (PR) badges.

4. **`screenshots/screenshot_4_analytics_charts_1080x1920.png`**
   - **Title**: Analytics & Volume Progression
   - **Highlights**: Total monthly volume lifted (`58,400 kg` with +12% indicator), 8-week volume chart, estimated 1RM calculations (Epley formula), consistency calendar.

5. **`screenshots/screenshot_5_routines_splits_1080x1920.png`**
   - **Title**: Custom Routines & Training Splits
   - **Highlights**: Push / Pull / Legs and Full Body training splits, estimated workout durations, '+ New Routine' builder.

6. **`screenshots/screenshot_6_body_privacy_1080x1920.png`**
   - **Title**: Body Tracking & 100% Offline Privacy
   - **Highlights**: Body weight trend, circumference measurement tracking (Chest, Waist, Arms), Metric/Imperial toggle, and zero cloud requirement.
